How To Play

Objective

Pick up passengers (green marker) and drop them at their destination (blue marker) in the taxi. Earn $400 per job. Buy safehouses across the city.

Controls — Driving

Note: if a passenger ever spawns outside the map, just reload the page and try again.

  • W / ↑ — Accelerate
  • S / ↓ — Brake & Reverse
  • A / ← — Steer Left
  • D / → — Steer Right
  • E — Exit Vehicle. It stays parked exactly where you left it — walk back up any time to get back in.
  • M — Return to Menu

Controls — On Foot

  • W A S D — Walk
  • Mouse — Look / Aim (click canvas to lock)
  • LMB — Fire Laser
  • E — Enter the nearest parked vehicle
  • F — Enter / exit a safehouse you own
  • Esc — Release mouse lock

Safehouses

Drive up to a safehouse marker and it auto-purchases when you have enough money. Each one comes with its own parked car, and its own walk-in interior once you own it. Each safehouse earns $25 passive income every 30 seconds — own all eight for $200/30s on top of your fares.

Districts

North · Downtown · Business · Suburbs · Alderna — a banner appears when you cross district boundaries. A river cuts through the middle of the map (roads bridge across it at 3 points), the far bottom-left corner is an industrial district of warehouses, and the eastern suburbs have residential neighborhoods with quieter, narrower streets.

Tips

  • Drive on roads only — buildings stop you dead
  • Traffic follows lanes — they'll block you at intersections
  • Only the taxi picks up fares — other cars are for getting around
  • Dev console password is for developers only!
